Privacy Policy

Al-Ājurrūmiyyah (الآجرومية) for iPhone · Last updated: 15 November 2026

This app teaches Arabic grammar through the text of al-Ājurrūmiyyah. It is designed to work without collecting any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not send any data to us or to any third party.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your lesson progress, study streak, quiz scores, reminder time, your light/dark mode choice and whether you have unlocked the full course. This is kept on your iPhone and is deleted when you delete the app. The app's Home Screen widget reads the same progress from your iPhone.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, your lesson progress, streak and quiz scores are also saved to your own iCloud account so they carry over to your other devices. This uses Apple's iCloud service; we cannot see or access it. You can turn it off for this app in Settings › [your name] › iCloud.

Reminders

If you turn on daily reminders, they are scheduled by your iPhone itself. Nothing is sent to us or to any server.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchase that unlocks the full course is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
